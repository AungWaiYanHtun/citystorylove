CITYSTORYLOVE - DIRECTIONAL WALKING UPDATE

Preserves the original island map, buildings, UI, resources, save logic, and
four character identities. Adds eight cleaned directional walking frames per
character (two poses each for front, back, left, and right) and selects the
matching facing direction from movement.

NPC movement uses A* over a walkable-land mask for the lawn, sandy path, and
dock area. It avoids mapped natural obstacle zones, placed buildings, water,
and outer foliage. Diagonal corner cutting is disabled. Every grid-to-grid
movement segment is checked at short intervals, and intermediate route cells
are retained so path smoothing cannot shortcut through obstacles.

Checks performed:
- JavaScript syntax check with Node.js 22.
- ZIP archive integrity test.
- Confirmed all 32 directional sprite files exist and decode as valid PNGs.
- Pathfinding harness tested all 276 pairs of the island's 24 patrol nodes;
  every returned route segment stayed inside the walkable mask.

Not performed: a full live browser/mobile play-through. Please test the updated
ZIP on a staging copy of the game before replacing the live deployment.
