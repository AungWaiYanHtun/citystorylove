CITYSTORYLOVE — ISLAND CAMERA & RESIDENT WALKING REPAIR

This update is based on the uploaded citystorylove-natural-walking-repair.zip.
It preserves the existing island ground artwork, buildings, resources, menus,
save data, and the four original resident designs.

WHAT CHANGED
- The square island now fits the shorter viewport edge and is centered, so the
  complete island is visible on portrait and landscape phone screens.
- The camera refits after screen rotation or viewport resize.
- Residents use their original full front/back/left/right directional PNGs.
  The old synthetic split-leg clipping and duplicate sprite overlays are gone.
- The two supplied poses for each direction alternate based on distance moved,
  not render FPS, so the gait remains tied to actual walking speed.
- Resident scale is proportional to the fitted island camera.
- Walking speed is tuned to 76 world pixels per second.
- Existing walkable-land polygons, mapped natural obstacles, placed-building
  collision checks, A* pathfinding, diagonal corner checks, and safe route
  smoothing are retained.
- Modified script/style cache versions are bumped in index.html.

ASSETS
- 32 directional PNGs are included: 4 residents × 4 directions × 2 poses.
- Original island artwork and existing game assets are retained.

CHECKS PERFORMED
- `node --check island-game.js` passed.
- All 32 directional PNG files were found and decoded successfully.
- Pathfinding harness: 19 valid patrol points, all 342 ordered routes checked;
  no missing routes and no returned segment crossed a non-walkable area.
- 30 random resident spawn points were checked; all were walkable.
- ZIP integrity test passed.

NOT TESTED
- A complete live Android / Telegram / Netlify play-through was not performed.
  Test on a staging deployment before replacing the live site.
